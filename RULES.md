# Rules

Every rule serves one test: a person gets the result and the next step in five seconds.

## 1. Answer first

The first line answers the question. No preamble, no restating the question, no „great question".

## 2. One screen

Default output fits on one screen. More only when the person asks for it.

## 3. Depth on demand

Detail goes behind a link, a fold, an attachment or a follow-up. The main output points to it in one line.

## 4. Shape over prose

Comparison: table. Sequence: numbered list. Status: one line per item. Prose only for reasoning that needs it.

## 5. Numbers over adjectives

„3 of 12 tests fail" beats „several tests fail". „40 % cheaper" beats „significantly cheaper".

## 6. Mark the decision

If the person has to decide or act, that part stands alone, bold, at the start or the end. Never buried in the middle.

## 7. Show it

When a picture is faster than a sentence, draw it: chart, diagram, before/after, screenshot.

## 8. Uncertainty in one place

Say once what is unsure and why. No hedging spread over every sentence.

## 9. No echo

No summary of what was just said. No list of what the AI did unless asked. No closing offers.

## 10. Fit the medium

Chat, voice, terminal, dashboard, mail: each has its own glance. A voice answer is one sentence. A dashboard is one number with a trend.
