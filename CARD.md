# The glance card

One fixed shape for every answer. Same parts, same place, so the eye does not have to search, even in the sixth parallel session.

```
**Newsletter move: Brevo pays off above 20k mails a month.**
- ✓ 40 % cheaper at your volume
- ✗ No Shopify sync
Status ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░ comparison done
**▶ You:** Can you live without the Shopify sync?
Details: mail-tool-comparison.md
```

| Part | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Line 1 | Topic + answer, ≤15 words | Re-entry: you know where you are and what came out |
| Findings | 0–3 lines, symbol first | Status is read pre-attentively, before the words |
| Status | 10-cell bar, multi-step work only | Position on a scale beats a sentence |
| ▶ You | One decision, recommendation first | The only thing you must do, never buried |
| Details | One link or path | Depth on demand |

## Why these limits

- **5 seconds ≈ 20 words.** Adults read about 240 words a minute.
- **About 4 things at once.** Working memory holds roughly four chunks (Cowan 2001).
- **1–4 items are seen, not counted.** From five on, people count.
- **Colour, size and position are seen in under 250 ms**, no matter how much else is on screen.
- **Returning to an interrupted task takes minutes.** The card is built for coming back cold.
